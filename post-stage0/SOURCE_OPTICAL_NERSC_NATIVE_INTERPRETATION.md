# Source-only NERSC native-grid probe interpretation

Status: source-side validation note only. No WALLABY validation kinematics or Gate-A/B response values are used here.

## Question

The direct DR10 multi-brick optical probe used a source-centred output grid with bilinear reprojection. A remaining source-side nuisance question was whether the recovered optical quantities were materially driven by interpolation/resampling of the containing Legacy Survey brick.

## Native-primary-grid test

The native-grid probe defines the 1024-pixel target WCS as an exact integer-pixel extension of the geometrically containing DR10 brick. Pixels owned by the containing brick are copied with no interpolation. Only pixels geometrically owned by the neighbouring brick are bilinearly reprojected; there is no overlap averaging.

For WALLABY J133209-245132 (NGC 5044 TR3), the target footprint intersects two DR10 bricks. The containing brick supplies 746496 of 1048576 pixels per band by exact native integer-pixel assignment; the neighbour supplies 302080 pixels per band by bilinear reprojection. The final g/r/z mosaics are fully finite and the common optical aperture is fully contained.

Relative to the earlier source-centred bilinear NERSC result, the native-primary-grid result changes:

- R50,r by +0.04257 arcsec;
- stellar exponential-equivalent scale Rd,* by +0.01367 kpc;
- log10(Mstar) by +0.00620 dex.

The native result is R50,r = 15.34685 arcsec, Rd,* = 4.92835 kpc, and log10(Mstar) = 10.86595.

Relative to the legacy viewer-based 1024 reference, the native result differs by -0.83747 arcsec in R50,r and -0.26894 kpc in Rd,* while log10(Mstar) differs by only -0.00021 dex. The common uncertainty aperture is 20.27 arcsec smaller in the native direct-NERSC construction.

## Decision

The direct-NERSC optical result is not materially generated by primary-brick interpolation. The stellar-mass shift caused by removing interpolation from the containing brick is substantially below the frozen 0.02-dex source-side convergence tolerance, and the R50 shift is substantially below the frozen 0.5-arcsec tolerance. This closes the specific primary-grid resampling debt.

It does **not** by itself earn Stage-1 or authorize opening validation kinematics. Stage-1 still requires the completed source-only predictor table, baryonic H I fold table, optical/stellar metadata, beam/recovery report, hashes, and external timestamp required by the Stage-0A implementation lock.
